# Assumptions Log

Template content for the configured OpenProject Wiki Assumptions Log. Every entry must also be linked from the OpenProject WP and posted in its dedicated Buzz thread.

## Template entry

```markdown
### [WP-#ID] Target: <Module/File>
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Dependent On:** <missing interface / package / WP>
- **Assumption Made:** <signature, stub type, payload, behavior>
- **Reconciliation Action:** <what must be proven at integration>
- **Status:** open | reconciled | waived
```
